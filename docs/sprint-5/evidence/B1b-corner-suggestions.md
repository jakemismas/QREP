## Method

Inputs: the v0 eval set's tier A rows (`eval_set` v0 in `corpus/manifest.csv`), minus the
holdout (the corpus README's rule, applied through `qrep_eval.annotation.in_holdout` before any
annotation or image is read): 11 museum scans, decoded with cv2 from the main checkout's
private cache. The 2 holdout photos are counted, not named. Each proposal pass's outer edge,
field corners, bands and counts are the truth it is scored against.

For each photo and each cap:

1. Stage the whole photo, since no corner is known yet: its long side capped at 1400 px
   (phone) or 2000 px (desktop), the caps in `web/src/model/downscale.ts`, with area
   resampling.
2. Run each method on the staged photo and scale its corners back to source pixels.
3. Score each corner against the proposed outer-edge corner: the distance over the square size,
   which is the field's mean edge lengths over the counts, or, for the 2 photos with no field
   corners (and no bands, so the outer edge is the field), the outer edge's over the counts.

The methods:

- backdrop_hull: the image in L*0.5 Lab after a 1 px blur. Each side of a border ring (2
  percent of the shorter side) is uniform when its MAD is at most 4 units and at least 90
  percent of it lies within max(6, 4 MADs) of its median; two uniform sides are needed. Pixels
  within a uniform side's tolerance and connected to the photo's border are the backdrop. The
  rest, opened by 0.5 percent of the shorter side, gives its largest component, which must
  cover 5 to 98 percent of the photo and touch at most 2 percent of any photo side. Its outline's
  convex hull, simplified to four corners (`_quad_from_contour`), must overlap the component by
  at least 0.9 (intersection over union), and that overlap is the confidence.
- backdrop_lines: the same component, with a Huber line fit to each side's outline points
  (the end 10 percent of each side left out) and the corners where neighbouring lines meet.
- tiered: tiers 0 to 2 of `qrep/vision/rectify.py` `_detect_tiered`, copied at c8e4f8a, with
  tier 3's full frame recorded as no suggestion.
- Band proposals: the photo warped from the proposed outer edge to a rectangle, unblurred, and
  each side's mean color across its middle 60 percent at each depth down to a quarter of the
  shorter side. Change points are peaks of the difference between the mean color of the window
  after a depth and the window before it (1 percent of the shorter side), at least 2 units, at
  least a window apart, at most 4 a side. The true band edges are the running sums of the band
  widths, with the innermost (the field's edge) taken from the field corners, because one
  width per band cannot say a border is deeper on two sides (lacma-54904).

Percentiles are linear interpolation over the corners of photos with a suggestion. Every knob
is a named constant with its source at the top of `scripts/eval/spikes/b1b_corners.py`, and
the helpers, the rule and the methods have hand-worked tests in `tests/eval/test_b1b_scoring.py`.

The copy of the old detector was checked once against the module itself, before B6b deletes it,
from this branch at 79d7581 with the worktree's venv. For each of the 22 staged dev images (11
photos at both caps), the script below compared the copy's `_detect_tiered` with
`qrep.vision.rectify._detect_tiered`. Quads, residuals, tiers and confidences were identical on
22 of 22, with tier 3 on 16 and tier 2 on 6, as the tiered rows here show. The check is not
committed, because it imports the module the spike must not need.

```python
photos, _skipped = b1b.load_photos()
for photo in photos:
    image = cv2.imread(str(cache / Path(photo.file).name), cv2.IMREAD_COLOR)
    for cap in b1b.CAPS.values():
        staged, _scale = b1b.stage(image, cap)
        a, b = b1b._detect_tiered(staged), rectify._detect_tiered(staged)
        assert np.array_equal(a[0], b[0]) and a[1:] == b[1:]
```
